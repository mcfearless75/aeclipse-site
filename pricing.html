Pricing

Transparent, Bespoke Pricing

The auto enrolment industry doesn't do one-size-fits-all — and neither do we. We'd be delighted to discuss your business' requirements and tailor a quote that works.

Why bespoke?

Different employer counts, different formats, different services. A flat price would be unfair to half our clients. We tailor a quote so you only pay for what you need — and so we leave room for your own margin if you're a partner firm.

  • No setup fees
  • No long-term contract lock-in
  • Per pay-period or per-employer pricing
  • Plenty of margin for partner firms
