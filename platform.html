For Accountants & Bureaus

How AEclipse works for you

AEclipse saves accountants and payroll bureaus time and money by enabling integration of payroll with any pension provider — without the need for development or programming.

We've created a simple mapping process so we convert CSV files to XML format seamlessly, without disruption to your business or your clients.

Whether we set up schemes, run pay-period assessments and produce letters, or simply act as a conduit for your data — the choice is yours, and the charges leave room for your own margin.

What we cover

  • Scheme set up with any pension provider
  • Pay-period assessments
  • Mandatory communications and letters
  • Staff enrolment and re-enrolment
  • Declarations of compliance
  • Post-staging ongoing management
