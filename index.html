About AEclipse

Built by experts, trusted by hundreds

AEclipse was founded in 2014 to ease the burden of auto enrolment for UK accountants and employers. We now look after hundreds of accountants and payroll bureaus across the UK.

Widely acknowledged as one of the best auto enrolment middleware providers on the market, we enable everyone to deal with any pension provider regardless of their payroll software.
